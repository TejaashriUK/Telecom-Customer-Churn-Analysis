Payment Method Analysis:
1. Electronic check users have the highest churn rate (45.31%).



2. Credit card automatic payments have the lowest churn rate (15.24%).



3. Automatic payment users show lower churn than electronic check users.



4. The company should investigate why electronic check users are leaving and encourage automatic payment adoption.

Tenure Group Analysis:

1. Customers with 0–1 year of tenure have the highest churn rate (47.73%).

2. Customers with 4+ years of tenure have the lowest churn rate (9.51%).

3. Churn generally decreases as customer tenure increases.

4. The company should focus on retaining newly acquired customers through onboarding support, engagement and loyalty offers.

Monthly Charges Analysis:

1. Customers with high monthly charges have the highest churn rate (35.48%).

2. Customers with low monthly charges have the lowest churn rate (10.86%).

3. Churn increases as the monthly charge category increases.

4. The company should review pricing plans and offer suitable packages to customers with high monthly charges.
